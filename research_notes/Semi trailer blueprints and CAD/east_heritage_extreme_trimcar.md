# Public blueprints, dimensional data, patents and CAD software: East Manufacturing (East Trailers), "Heritage", Extreme Trailers, "Trimcar"

Research date: 2026-10-30. Only public, ungated pages were used. Notes on method:
- "Verified" means I fetched the page or PDF myself; for PDFs I also pulled the text out with pdftotext.
- "Snippet" means the fact came only from a search-result summary. I did not open the page, so treat those as unverified.
- tremcar.com returns a reCAPTCHA "Bot Verification" page to automated fetches, so I could not read Tremcar's own site.

## Key Question 1: Who are the four companies? (identity resolution)

### Takeaway
- **East Manufacturing** is confirmed. It has been renamed East Trailers LLC and is based in Randolph, OH. "BST" is its BEAST flatbed and drop-deck family.
- **Extreme Trailers** is confirmed as Extreme Trailers, LLC of Dover, OH, at extremetrailersllc.com. Do not confuse it with extremetrailers.com, a boat-trailer company.
- **"Trimcar"** matches no company. The closest real name is **Tremcar Inc.**, a Québec tank-trailer maker. There is a real link to the user's list: a Québec dealer carries both Extreme and Tremcar, and an Extreme manager came from Tremcar. If the user meant a flatbed maker, the only plausible match is **Transcraft**, now sold as Wabash.
- **"Heritage"** matches no maker of semi-trailer flatbeds. The only literal match is Heritage Trailers LLC / Heritage Custom Trailers in Benton, IL. Its federal registration covers light and medium "Trailer and Marine Trailers", not 80,000-lb semi flatbeds. The name is most likely misheard. Ask the user.
