# NE Ohio Semi-Trailer Manufacturers: Documented History, Connections, and Identity of "Trim Car"

Scope note: Everything below has an inline source. Items marked **[snippet]** were seen only in a search-engine result summary and the page itself was not opened. Items marked **[aggregator]** come from a people-data or company-data aggregator rather than a primary source. Treat both as unverified. Research date: 2026-10-30.

## Q1. Who is "Trim Car"? (identification)

### Takeaway
"Trim Car" is **Tremcar**, through its U.S. subsidiary **Tremcar USA Inc.** (the coordinator relayed the user's confirmation that voice-to-text garbled the name). Tremcar USA has had its head office and main plant in **Strasburg, Ohio** (Tuscarawas County) since 2004. It also ran a second plant in **Dover, Ohio** that built aluminum crude-oil tank trailers. Tremcar vacated the Dover plant during the 2015–2016 oil-and-gas downturn. **Extreme Trailers LLC was then started in that same Dover building by Les Smith, Tremcar's former vice president of operations**, using workers who already had Tremcar experience. That is the documented MAC → Tremcar → Extreme link the user remembers.

### Cited Findings
**Tremcar parent company**
- Tremcar Inc. is a Québec tank-trailer maker. The NHTSA manufacturer record gives its address as 790 Avenue Montrichard, St-Jean-sur-Richelieu, Québec, and lists DBAs Tremcar Industries Inc., Tremcar West Inc. and Asybco. Its WMI is 2TL. — [NHTSA vPIC, Tremcar Inc. (ID 19927)](https://vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov/decoder/Manufacturer/Details/19927)
- Aldé and Léo Tougas founded the business as A&L Tougas in 1962 in Sabrevois, Québec, to build milk tanks. Jacques Tremblay bought it in 1989 and renamed it Tremcar. His son Daniel Tremblay joined in 1992 and is now president. — [Trailer/Body Builders, "Elemental passion: Tremcar expands U.S. operations," Jul. 24, 2025](https://www.trailer-bodybuilders.com/whats-in-print/article/55284745/elemental-passion-tremcar-expands-us-operations)
  - Conflict: another source says the Tougas brothers sold the company to Jacques Tremblay and a partner in **1987**, not 1989. **[snippet]** — [Bulk Transporter, "Tremcar celebrates 60 years"](https://bulktransporter.com/21234979)
- Tremcar describes itself as the largest family-owned tank-trailer maker in North America. — [Tank Transport, Oct. 9, 2019](https://tanktransport.com/2019/10/tremcar-growing-u-s-presence/)

**Tremcar in the U.S. and in Ohio**
- Tremcar's first U.S. plant opened in Champlain, New York, in 1998 and closed in December 2000. — [Tank Transport, Oct. 9, 2019](https://tanktransport.com/2019/10/tremcar-growing-u-s-presence/)
- In 2004 Tremcar moved its U.S. operation into the former **International Tank Trailer** facility in Ohio. — [Trailer/Body Builders, Jul. 24, 2025](https://www.trailer-bodybuilders.com/whats-in-print/article/55284745/elemental-passion-tremcar-expands-us-operations)
- On Dec. 27, 2004, trade press reported that Tremcar USA Inc. had opened a 31,000-sq-ft manufacturing and repair center in Strasburg, OH. Shane Walters was plant manager. — [Bulk Transporter, "Tremcar USA opens new center in Ohio," Dec. 27, 2004](https://www.bulktransporter.com/archive/article/21639891/tremcar-usa-opens-new-center-in-ohio)
- The 2004 Strasburg site started as Tremcar USA's first U.S. service center. It was later turned into an aluminum-tank plant for crude and refined oil. As of 2019 it had more than 100 employees and ran a rental fleet of 350 tank trailers. Annie Tremblay became executive vice president in 2011 and moved to Strasburg to run Tremcar USA. Tremcar also bought Boston Steel, a fuel-delivery tank-truck maker, in 2014. — [Tank Transport, Oct. 9, 2019](https://tanktransport.com/2019/10/tremcar-growing-u-s-presence/)
- The Strasburg production facility is at 436 12th St NE. A grand opening for a new cargo-tank production building there was held Sept. 19, 2019. — [Bulk Transporter, Jul. 17, 2019](https://bulktransporter.com/equipment/cargo-tanks-trailers/article/21658099/tremcar-opening-new-tank-facility-in-strasburg-oh)
- In 2023 Strasburg added a $4M, 20,000-sq-ft building for DOT 406 tank trailers, running since mid-October 2023. A Strasburg service building opened in 2018 and was expanded for $1.5M. Dave Brooks was general manager of Tremcar USA. — [Bulk Transporter, Nov. 30, 2023](https://www.bulktransporter.com/equipment/cargo-tanks-trailers/article/21278264/tremcar-expands-us-tank-trailer-production-capacity)
- In 2025 Strasburg staff included Dave Brooks (plant manager), Jim Everett (service manager) and Kevin Vogel (QC manager). The plant built 134 aluminum DOT 406 tankers in 2023 and 172 in 2024. — [Trailer/Body Builders, Jul. 24, 2025](https://www.trailer-bodybuilders.com/whats-in-print/article/55284745/elemental-passion-tremcar-expands-us-operations)

**Tremcar's Dover, Ohio plant (the link to Extreme Trailers)**
- On Dec. 2, 2015, Bulk Transporter reported that Tremcar's **Dover, Ohio** plant had been designed to build aluminum crude-oil cargo tanks for North Dakota and Western Canada and had been hit hard by falling oil prices. Tremcar was reorganizing and consolidating its Dover and Strasburg plants, and Dover's future was undecided. Annie Tremblay, EVP, managed both plants. — [Bulk Transporter, "Tremcar addresses impact of oilfield slowdown on U.S. plants," Dec. 2, 2015](https://www.bulktransporter.com/equipment/cargo-tanks-trailers/article/21654929/tremcar-addresses-impact-of-oilfield-slowdown-on-us-plants)
- A business listing places Tremcar USA Inc. at **317 E Broadway St, Dover, OH 44622**. **[snippet]** — [MapQuest listing](https://www.mapquest.com/us/ohio/tremcar-usa-inc-446615970)
- NHTSA lists **Extreme Trailers LLC at 317 East Broadway, Dover, Ohio 44622**, which is the same address. — [NHTSA vPIC, Extreme Trailers LLC (ID 15165)](https://vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov/mid/manufacturer/details/15165)
- Extreme's Dover plant is a 100,000-sq-ft former Tremcar plant that Tremcar vacated during the oil-and-gas bust. Les Smith "previously served as vice president of operations at Tremcar." He said he worked with the Economic Development and Finance Alliance (EDFA) and Tremcar to acquire the facility, along with a plasma table, shear and overhead cranes. The facility runs under a 10-year agreement with EDFA. Many workers were already on site with Tremcar trailer-building and aluminum-welding experience, and materials manager Shawn Ellwood previously worked at Tremcar. — [Trailer/Body Builders, "Extreme Trailers pushes the limits of light," Sept. 5, 2017](https://www.trailer-bodybuilders.com/trailers/extreme-trailers-pushes-limits-light)
- Les Smith's career, per an aggregator profile: VP of Supply Chain at **MAC Trailer** (2014), then VP of Operations (USA) at **Tremcar** (Aug. 2014 – Sept. 2015), based in Dover, OH. **[aggregator + snippet; the page itself returned 403]** — [ContactOut profile](https://contactout.com/les-smith-96234). A LinkedIn result for a Les Smith in Dover, OH, lists him as President/CEO of Extreme Trailers LLC. **[snippet]** — [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/les-smith-53453b41/)

### Inferences
- "Trim Car" = Tremcar / Tremcar USA Inc. The confidence is very high. The user confirmed the name, both Tremcar Ohio sites (Strasburg and Dover) are in Tuscarawas County, and Extreme Trailers occupies Tremcar's former Dover plant at the same street address.
- If the ContactOut dates are right, Les Smith went from MAC Trailer to Tremcar in 2014 and then founded Extreme in 2016. That is exactly the MAC → Tremcar → Extreme order the user described. Only the MAC → Tremcar step rests on an aggregator. The Tremcar → Extreme step is confirmed by trade press.
- Tremcar is a tank-trailer maker, not a dump/flatbed maker like East, Trailstar, MAC and Extreme. In the user's lineage it is a step in one person's career and a building, not the same product line. Extreme took over the Dover plant's aluminum-welding workforce and equipment.

### Gaps
- No source found gives the **date Tremcar opened** the Dover plant. Likely the early-2010s shale boom, but this is not documented. No source gives the exact date it closed (somewhere between Dec. 2015 and mid-2016 is implied).
- Nothing found on the history or owners of **International Tank Trailer**, the earlier occupant of the Strasburg site.
- Les Smith's MAC tenure and title rest only on an aggregator. No MAC press release or news item confirms it.
- Ohio Secretary of State filings were not retrieved. The site was not queried directly, so there are no charter numbers or registration dates for Tremcar USA Inc. or Extreme Trailers LLC.

## Q2. Founders, founding dates, locations and documented prior employment (East, Trailstar, MAC, Extreme)

### Takeaway
East (1968, Randolph) was founded by Howard D. Booher, Mark Tate and Robert Bruce. Trailstar (1980, Alliance) has no documented founder. MAC (Alliance) was founded by Michael A. "Mike" Conny as a repair shop in 1992 and began building trailers around 1993–94. Extreme Trailers (Dover, 2016) was founded and is owned by Les Smith. The only founder-to-company link that is documented is **Smith's Tremcar background**, plus an aggregator-only MAC stint. **No source documents that Conny, or any Trailstar founder, came from East or Trailstar.**

### Cited Findings
**East Manufacturing (now East Trailers LLC), Randolph, OH**
- Founded in 1968 by Howard D. Booher, Mark Tate and Robert Bruce. Booher was CEO for 54 years, Bruce later became CEO, and the two were co-owners. Booher was a dairy farmer and aluminum welder, and the first product was an aluminum dump trailer. The company is named for the "East Ohio" area. It started with 10 employees in a 7,200-sq-ft building. — [Trailer/Body Builders, Mar. 17, 2022](https://trailer-bodybuilders.com/trailers/article/21236790/smoothsided-easts-booher-leaves-dream-legacy)
- Booher died March 14, 2022, at home in Randolph. **[snippet]** — [East news release (eastmfg.com)](https://eastmfg.com/?p=8988); [Trucknews](https://www.trucknews.com/transportation/east-mourns-death-of-ceo-co-owner-booher/1003157849)
- Address: 1871 State Route 44, Randolph, OH 44265. — [East "History of Firsts" page](https://www.east-trailers.com/history-of-firsts)
- In August 2023 Fultra, the Mexico-based parent of Fruehauf, acquired East Manufacturing. Terms were not disclosed. **[snippet]** — [Trailer/Body Builders](https://www.trailer-bodybuilders.com/trailers/article/21271801/fruehauf-parent-fultra-buys-east-manufacturing); [Business Wire, Aug. 15, 2023](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20230815248774/en/Fultra%C2%AE-parent-company-of-Fruehauf%C2%AE-Expands-Trailer-Footprint-in-North-America-with-the-Acquisition-of-East%C2%AE-Manufacturing)
- In January 2025 East Manufacturing LLC was renamed **East Trailers LLC**, and Tom Wiseman was quoted as president. **[snippet]** — [Trailer/Body Builders](https://trailer-bodybuilders.com/trailers/article/55260662/east-rebrands-to-emphasize-trailer-focus); [Trucknews](https://www.trucknews.com/transportation/east-manufacturing-rebrands-as-east-trailers/1003192804)
- A JobsOhio page names Chris Olson as President & CEO of East Trailers LLC. **[snippet]** — [JobsOhio](https://www.jobsohio.com/newsroom/news-press/east-trailers-accelerates-growth-with-new-manufacturing-line-expansion)

**Trailstar International, Alliance, OH**
- Founded in 1980 in Alliance in a rented building. Its own plant at 20700 Harrisburg-Westville Rd, Alliance, was built in 1982. Tom Hahn started there sweeping floors in 1983 and "purchased Trailstar in 2012." He was president at the time of the article, with Mike Schmidt (plant manager), John Barker (sales manager) and Heather Dibert (controller). No founders are named. — [Movin' Out, "Trailstar International Built With Pride In The USA" (c. 2016)](https://www.movinout.com/magazine/article/trailstar-international-built-with-pride-in-the-usa-30.php)
- Trailstar says it has been "manufacturing and distributing reliable dump trailers since 1980." Same Alliance address. No founders or ownership stated. — [Trailstar About page](https://www.trailstarintl.com/about)

**MAC Trailer Mfg., Inc., Alliance, OH**
- Michael A. Conny "had started in the trailer-building industry" and then began wreck-and-repair work in a one-bay garage, funded by a small loan from his mother. The company history says he founded MAC Trailer in **1994** and built its first dump trailer that year. **The page does not name his earlier employer.** — [MAC Trailer history page](https://www.mactrailer.com/our-company/history)
- A 2005 profile says Conny opened a trailer repair business in **1992**, with a loan from his mother and stepfather, as the only employee in a single-bay garage. A repair customer asked him to build a trailer and then ordered 20 more, and within seven months he had 10 employees. — [Smart Business, Apr. 22, 2005](https://sbnonline.com/article/happy-trail-er-s-how-mac-trailer-stayed-ahead-of-the-competition)
- In July 2013 MAC was "celebrating 20 years of manufacturing," which implies about 1993. — [Trailer/Body Builders, Jul. 3, 2013](https://www.trailer-bodybuilders.com/trailers/mac-trailer-celebrates-20-years-manufacturing)
- NHTSA lists Michael A. Conny as president, at 14599 Commerce St., Alliance, OH 44601. Related entities include MAC Manufacturing, MAC Waste Trailer, MAC LTT, MAC Trailer Service, MAC Trailer of Oklahoma, MAC Trailer Texas, MAC Trailer Iowa, Beall Manufacturing and **Trailstar International, Inc.** Trade names are MAC Trailer, MAC LTT, Beall, Trailstar and MAC Bullet. — [NHTSA vPIC, MAC Trailer Mfg (ID 5950)](https://vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov/mid/manufacturer/details/5950)
- Milestones: 2002 aluminum tipper; 2004 moving-floor waste trailer; 2009 dry bulk tanks; 2011 MAC Liquid Tank Trailer (LTT) line launched in **Kent, OH**; 2013 acquired Trailstar; 2014 Davis, OK; 2021 Beall (Portland, OR); 2022 Bullet Mfg (Sunnyside, WA); 12 facilities in 6 states as of 2025. — [MAC Trailer history page](https://www.mactrailer.com/our-company/history)

**Extreme Trailers LLC, Dover, OH**
- Owner and principal: Les Smith, at 317 East Broadway, Dover, OH 44622. NHTSA submittal dated 6/14/2016, production start 07/18/2016. Class 8 trailers, WMI 7VG. — [NHTSA vPIC (ID 15165)](https://vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov/mid/manufacturer/details/15165)
- Les Smith is president and CEO. The company secured its manufacturing space in May 2016, began with five employees and quickly tripled. It held an open house and ribbon-cutting in February 2017. — [Trailer/Body Builders, Feb. 28, 2017](https://www.trailer-bodybuilders.com/trailers/article/21741623/extreme-trailer-holds-open-house)
- Smith has 30+ years in dump, refuse, drop-deck, flatbed and dry-box trailers and was previously VP of operations at Tremcar. First trailers came off the line in January 2017. Products include the X-Lite, X-Caliber and X-Force flatbeds, aluminum drop decks, and aluminum and steel dumps. Don Hayes (VP operations) came from Hendrickson and Freightliner, and Ryan Rummell (engineering) came from Kimble Manufacturing. — [Trailer/Body Builders, Sept. 5, 2017](https://www.trailer-bodybuilders.com/trailers/extreme-trailers-pushes-limits-light)

### Inferences
- MAC's founding date depends on the definition. Repair business: 1992 (SBN 2005). First manufactured trailers: about 1993 (TBB's "20 years" in 2013). Official company history: 1994. The user's "1992 or 1994" matches the documented range.
- MAC's own history says Conny was "in the trailer-building industry" before 1992. Given the Alliance location, a prior job at East or Trailstar is plausible, but **no source names his employer**, so the East → Trailstar → MAC "people moved" chain is **undocumented** in public sources.

### Gaps
- No public source names Trailstar's 1980 founders, or any founder link between Trailstar and East.
- No source documents Mike Conny's employer before 1992.
- No source documents Les Smith's employers before 2014 beyond "30+ years" across trailer types.

## Q3. Did Trailstar have an earlier name before 1980, and who owned it before MAC's 2013 acquisition?

### Takeaway
No earlier name was found. Trailstar's own materials date it to 1980 in Alliance. On ownership, the sources disagree: a Movin' Out profile (c. 2016) says Tom Hahn **bought Trailstar in 2012** and was president, while MAC says it **acquired Trailstar in 2013**. NHTSA now lists Trailstar International, Inc. as a MAC-related entity and Trailstar as a MAC trade name.

### Cited Findings
- Founded in 1980 in a rented Alliance building, with its own plant in 1982. Tom Hahn joined in 1983 and "purchased Trailstar in 2012." — [Movin' Out (c. 2016)](https://www.movinout.com/magazine/article/trailstar-international-built-with-pride-in-the-usa-30.php)
- "2013: MAC acquired Trailstar International of Alliance, Ohio," and MAC still builds Trailstar dump trailers. — [MAC Trailer history page](https://www.mactrailer.com/our-company/history)
- An Oct. 2016 article describes Trailstar's "resurgence back into the Trailer world," with 31 new dealerships added over the previous four years (about 2012–2016). — [Movin' Out, Oct. 2016](https://movinout.com/magazine/article/trailstar-international-parts-department-sales-service-and-a-whole-lot-more-422.php)
- NHTSA lists Trailstar International, Inc. among MAC's related entities and "Trailstar" among MAC's trade names. — [NHTSA vPIC (ID 5950)](https://vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov/mid/manufacturer/details/5950)
- One aggregator says MAC acquired Trailstar in 2013 and that the "TRAILSTAR" trademark is registered to MAC Trailer Manufacturing, Inc. **[aggregator]** — [Accio company analysis](https://www.accio.com/business/trailstar)

### Inferences
- The 2012 Hahn purchase and the 2013 MAC acquisition could both be true. For example, Hahn may have bought the company in 2012 and MAC acquired it or took a stake in 2013 while Hahn stayed on as president. The "resurgence" timing fits a 2012–13 recapitalization. This is speculation, and no source describes the deal's structure.

### Gaps
- No Alliance Review, Canton Repository or trade-press announcement of the 2012 or 2013 transactions was found.
- Owners from 1980 to 2012 are unknown, and so is any earlier name.

## Q4. Other trailer manufacturers in or spun out of the NE Ohio cluster

### Takeaway
Besides East, Trailstar, MAC and Extreme, the documented cluster members found are **Tremcar USA** (Strasburg plus the former Dover plant), its predecessor site **International Tank Trailer** (Strasburg), and **MAC LTT** (Kent, 2011). No other documented employee spin-offs were found.

### Cited Findings
- MAC LTT (liquid tank trailers) launched in Kent, OH, in 2011. — [MAC Trailer history page](https://www.mactrailer.com/our-company/history)
- Tremcar USA moved into the former International Tank Trailer facility in Ohio in 2004. — [Trailer/Body Builders, Jul. 24, 2025](https://www.trailer-bodybuilders.com/whats-in-print/article/55284745/elemental-passion-tremcar-expands-us-operations)
- Trade coverage also mentions Tremcar trailers listed for sale from Beach City, Ohio, without saying whether that is a plant or a dealer. **[snippet]** — [Machinery Trader listing](https://www.machinerytrader.com/listings/for-sale/tremcar/semi-trailers/880)

### Inferences
- The cluster splits into an aluminum dump/flatbed lineage (East, Trailstar, MAC, Extreme) and a tank-trailer lineage (International Tank Trailer → Tremcar USA, plus MAC LTT and MAC's dry-bulk line). The two meet at Les Smith and the Dover building.

### Gaps
- No systematic NHTSA vPIC export of Ohio trailer manufacturers by county was done. The Part 566 list is large and not searchable by county through the tools available.
- No documented founders or links were found for other small Stark, Portage, Columbiana, Tuscarawas or Mahoning trailer shops.

## Q5. Documented litigation between these companies

### Takeaway
**No lawsuit between any of these companies** (East, Trailstar, MAC, Tremcar, Extreme) was found in the public sources searched. The only court records that turned up are unrelated product-liability cases.

### Cited Findings
- *Chamberlain v. Mac Trailer Mfg., Inc.*, 128 A.D.3d 1336 (N.Y. App. Div. 4th Dept., May 1, 2015), 2015 NY Slip Op 03636. ### Inferences
- The absence of inter-company suits in search results is weak evidence. Trade-secret or non-compete cases are often filed in state court (e.g., Stark or Tuscarawas County Common Pleas) and may not be indexed by search engines.
