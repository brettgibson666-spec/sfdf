# MAC Trailer Manufacturing (Alliance, Ohio) and its brands: public blueprints, dimensioned specs, CAD models, and engineering software

Research date: 2026-10-30. "Verified" means I fetched the URL myself and it returned HTTP 200 with the stated content. PDF dates are taken from the PDF metadata (CreationDate) or from form numbers printed in the document. Every MAC-owned document below is on a public URL. No login was used.

**Main findings up front**
1. MAC's own marketing literature contains **no dimensioned general-arrangement (GA) line drawings**. Every flyer and brochure is photos plus text spec lists. Some of those lists include dimension callouts such as overall length, width, height, wheelbase from kingpin to axle, coupler or fifth-wheel height, and axle spread.
2. The best real engineering drawings in public are **nine MAC Manufacturing Inc. retrofit/add-on kit drawings** (part numbers 73803000–73803008, dated 2015–2019). They are hosted on MAC's aftermarket parts resources page. Each has a full title block, a tolerance block, exploded isometrics, ballooned parts lists, and weights in lbm.
3. Those drawings carry a **"SOLID EDGE HOME USE" watermark**, which is direct evidence of Siemens Solid Edge use around 2015–2019. **MAC's 2026 job postings require SolidWorks (2D and 3D), plus FEA and ERP.**
4. The patent drawings are the richest public source of structural trailer geometry. MAC publishes its own patent list of roughly 80+ entries.

## 1. Complete list of MAC Trailer brands, divisions and affiliates, and their websites

### Takeaway
The parent company is **MAC Trailer Enterprises, Inc.** in Alliance, OH. Its brands are MAC Trailer, MAC LTT (liquid tanks, now being folded into the MAC Trailer brand), Beall®, Trailstar®, MAC Bullet, MAD Trailer Leasing & Logistics, Aftermarket Parts, and MAC Trailer Service. Almost everything is hosted on **mactrailer.com**, a HubSpot site. Old brand domains (macltt.com, beallmfg.com) now redirect there. Trailstar keeps its own site, **trailstarintl.com**.

### Inferences
- "MAC Manufacturing Inc." is the legal entity name in the title blocks of the pneumatic kit drawings (Section 3). Per the c. 2020 entity list it is the Salem, OH dry-bulk pneumatic tank entity. This suggests those drawings came from the Salem pneumatic engineering group.
- The brand consolidation (MAC LTT folded into MAC Trailer, the Beall domain redirected) means older MAC LTT and Beall literature URLs may disappear. Archive them now if they are needed.

### Gaps
- macbullet.com returned HTTP 200 with an empty page and no title. I could not confirm that it is a MAC property.
- "MAC Trailer Iowa, Inc." appears as a DBA in NHTSA data, but I found no Iowa plant on MAC's site.

### Inferences
- The most precise public geometry in MAC literature is in the 550 Doubles, Bullet, Road Warrior, and MAC-1000/1050 flyers. Together these give kingpin-to-axle wheelbase, coupler/fifth-wheel height, OAL/OAW/OAH, and axle spread. A 2D side-elevation could be roughed out from them, but no MAC-drawn GA is published.
- MAC seems to treat GA and customer-approval drawings as private sales and engineering documents. The job postings mention "engineering review of customer specifications and final drawings."

### Gaps
- I did not page through the FlippingBook overview brochures visually. They may contain a dimension diagram that the text extraction missed. Sampled text showed no "overall length" or "kingpin" strings.
- No "spec sheet" (single-model GA with dimension callouts) exists on the public site for drop decks, MLP-8, half rounds, smoothsides, or liquid tanks.
