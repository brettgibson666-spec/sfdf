# Cross-cutting sources of semi-trailer blueprints, CAD, design standards, and trailer-industry engineering software (North America, as of Oct 2026)

Research date: 2026-10-30. "Verified" means the page or data was fetched or returned directly during this session. "Snippet" means the claim comes from a search-result summary whose underlying page was not opened, so treat it as lower confidence. Patent lists came from live Google Patents queries (patents.google.com/xhr/query) run on 2026-10-30.

## 1. Patents as a source of trailer structural drawings (Google Patents / USPTO / Espacenet)

### Takeaway
Patents are the richest free, legal source of manufacturer-specific structural drawings for North American semi-trailers. Every major flatbed, drop-deck, dump, and tank builder has granted patents with figures of frames, necks, floors, upper couplers, and guards. Search Google Patents by assignee name, and by CPC class to find work from smaller builders. Patent drawings show concepts and arrangements, not production dimensions or bills of materials.

### Cited Findings
**Search tools and URL patterns**
- Google Patents accepts assignee/CPC/country URL parameters. These queries returned results on 2026-10-30 (counts are US documents, applications plus grants):
  - `https://patents.google.com/?cpc=B62D53%2F06&country=US`: about 2,107 results. CPC B62D53/06 covers semi-trailers. — [Google Patents query](https://patents.google.com/?cpc=B62D53%2F06&country=US)
  - `https://patents.google.com/?cpc=B62D53%2F08&country=US`: about 3,820 results (fifth-wheel coupling / tractor-trailer combinations) — [Google Patents](https://patents.google.com/?cpc=B62D53%2F08&country=US)
  - `https://patents.google.com/?cpc=B62D21%2F20&country=US`: about 401 results (vehicle frames for trailers). Hits included East Mfg. US9399492B2 and MAC US10875587B2. — [Google Patents](https://patents.google.com/?cpc=B62D21%2F20&country=US)

### Inferences
- Querying by assignee works better than by keyword for the big OEMs. Some OEMs patent under individuals' names: Reitnouer patents are assigned to "Miles A. Reitnouer", and a recent Great Dane rear-frame application lists inventor John Joven Dabu Ubaña as the assignee. Search by inventor name too.
- Many short-line or regional builders did not return Google Patents hits under their company names in this session, including Doepker, Benson, Etnyre, Talbert, XL Specialized, and Landoll (some queries hit rate limits). Use CPC-class searches combined with keywords such as "drop deck", "lowboy", "end dump", and "tank trailer" to find their work.
- Patent figures are good for structural arrangement, such as neck reinforcement, crossmember layout, and kingpin plate construction. They usually lack production tolerances, material callouts, and full dimensions.

### Gaps
- B60P3/22 (tank vehicles) and B62D53/061 counts were not retrieved because of rate limits.

- No figure-quality review was done; drawing quality per patent is unverified.

## 2. Free/public CAD and 3D model libraries with semi-trailer models

### Takeaway
Public libraries offer mostly low-to-medium-detail visualization models, not manufacturing blueprints. GrabCAD has some engineering-format flatbed models (native CAD and STEP). 3DCADBrowser sells drop-deck, flatbed, and tanker models in STEP/IGES. Sketchfab, RenderHub, Fab, and Superhive are game- or render-grade meshes. Swept-path tools (Autodesk Vehicle Tracking, AutoTURN) carry trailer geometry for turning analysis only.

### Inferences
- For someone wanting "blueprints", GrabCAD (native CAD and STEP) and 3DCADBrowser (STEP/IGES) are the only libraries likely to give engineering-usable solids. Even there, the models are hobbyist reconstructions, not OEM data. Sketchfab, RenderHub, Fab, and Superhive models are meshes, suitable for visualization or game use only.
- Swept-path libraries (AASHTO WB-62/WB-67 design vehicles in Vehicle Tracking or AutoTURN) give only wheelbase, kingpin, overhang, and width geometry for turning studies. They are useful as a dimensional sanity check, not as structural drawings.

### Gaps
- Could not verify specific GrabCAD, 3D Warehouse, TraceParts, Thingiverse, CADdetails, or BIMobject trailer models because these sites blocked automated access or render client-side.
- No direct confirmation of which AASHTO trailer templates ship in the AutoTURN or Vehicle Tracking libraries.

## 4. Standards and dimensional references (SAE, TMC, TTMA, FMVSS/FMCSR, 23 CFR 658)

### Takeaway
Federal rules are free on eCFR: 23 CFR 658 for size limits, 49 CFR 571 for FMVSS 108/121/223/224, and 49 CFR 393 for FMCSR parts and accessories. The interface standards that fix kingpin and coupler geometry (SAE J700, J133, J560) are paid SAE documents. TMC RPs are sold as an annual manual, and TTMA RPs are association publications; both are paid or member-oriented. A few TMC position papers are free PDFs.

## 5. NHTSA/DOT filings (49 CFR 566, 565 VIN decoder, vPIC)

### Takeaway
NHTSA manufacturer filings do **not** contain drawings or detailed dimensions. Part 566 requires only name, address, and a description of vehicle types with approximate GVWR ranges. Part 565 VIN-decoder letters on vPIC define VIN position codes, which can encode attributes like trailer type, length, or axle count, but carry no engineering drawings.

### Cited Findings
- **49 CFR 566.5 requires only:**
  - (a) the manufacturer's full name;
  - (b) residence address and state of incorporation;
  - (c) a "description of each type of motor vehicle... including... the approximate ranges of gross vehicle weight ratings". For trailers, the description must be "specific enough also to indicate the types of use... such as 'tank trailer'".
  - Filing goes to https://vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov/. (Verified text via eCFR.) — [eCFR 49 CFR 566](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/subtitle-B/chapter-V/part-566)
- **vPIC** is NHTSA's public catalog for VIN decoding and the Manufacturer Information Database (MID). Its dataset is built from manufacturers' Part 565 submissions. — [vPIC](https://vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov/); [vPIC API](https://vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov/api/); [data.gov vPIC dataset](https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/nhtsa-product-information-catalog-and-vehicle-listing-vpic-modifier-db); [NHTSA vPIC/VIN presentation PDF](https://www.nhtsa.gov/sites/nhtsa.dot.gov/files/frenchik-vin-vpic.pdf)
- **Trailer-maker 565 letters are public in the MID module**, for example JBALL Fish Houses' VIN-decoding submission and Ver-Mac's WMI 1V9 reservation. (Snippet.) — [vPIC MID file example](https://vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov/mid/home/displayfile/c699ca3d-c4d2-43f6-b21a-5b5bf7fc1341); [vPIC cover letter example](https://vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov/mfrportal/home/coverletter/a13c389d-b323-41f7-a5c0-cc2d0cba3b72)
- **NHTSA Manufacturer Helpdesk:** manufacturerinfo@dot.gov, 1-888-399-3277. (Snippet.) — [vPIC](https://vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov/)
- **Other NHTSA public documents carry trailer component details:**
  - Hendrickson service bulletins filed with NHTSA ODI — [NHTSA TSB SB-10062260](https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/tsbs/2013/SB-10062260-7690.pdf)
  - Recall documents — [NHTSA recall RCMN-19V279](https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/rcl/2019/RCMN-19V279-5953.pdf)

### Inferences
- VIN-decoder letters are useful for finding which builders exist and their model or type taxonomy. Some builders encode length or axle configuration in VIN positions, which gives coarse dimensional data, but nothing at blueprint level.
- NHTSA recall and TSB PDFs sometimes include component sketches and part drawings; they are a minor, free supplementary source.

### Gaps
- "MIQ" as named in the brief could not be matched to a current NHTSA database. The closest is the vPIC Manufacturer Information Database (MID).
- No specific trailer 565 letter was opened to confirm whether length or axle codes appear.

### Cited Findings
- **Hale Trailer** hosts per-unit and per-series spec PDFs. One example lists kingpin 30 in from front, coupler height 48 in, and floor heights around 59.5 in front and 54 in rear. — [Hale spec PDF 032752](https://trailerimages.haletrailer.com/inv/docs/trailer/032752-2016MANAC16-1.pdf); [Hale series PDF](https://trailerimages.haletrailer.com/inv/docs/series/019916-019923-1.pdf); [Hale Trail King spec](https://trailerimages.haletrailer.com/inv/docs/trailer/031958-2014TRAILKNG-1.pdf)
- **TruckPaper and MachineryTrader** "print" listings expose spec fields such as kingpin setting and dimensions for new and used East, MAC, Trail King, Fontaine, Load King, and Trinity trailers. — [TruckPaper Fontaine Revolution](https://www.truckpaper.com/listing/for-sale/255309667/2015-fontaine-revolution-flatbed-trailers-semi-trailers?print=1); [TruckPaper MAC 39 ft](https://www.truckpaper.com/listing/for-sale/257526041/2027-mac-trailer-mfg-39-ft-tandem-axle?print=1); [TruckPaper Trail King 47 ft](https://www.truckpaper.com/listing/for-sale/255921523/2023-trail-king-47-ft-tri-axle?print=1)
- **Rogers Brothers Trailers'** online glossary states that its kingpins conform to SAE J700. — [Rogers glossary](https://rogerstrailers.com/contact/glossary/k)

### Inferences
- Combining listing spec sheets (kingpin setting, deck heights, axle spread) with patent figures and SAE J700 kingpin geometry lets someone build a credible reference CAD model without access to OEM drawings.

### Gaps
- No permitting service (oversize/overweight permit vendors) was found that publicly hosts trailer drawings; this was not researched in depth.
- No auction sites (Ritchie Bros./RB, IronPlanet) were verified for spec-sheet or drawing attachments.
