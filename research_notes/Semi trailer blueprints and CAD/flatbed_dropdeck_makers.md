# Public Blueprints, Dimensioned Drawings and CAD from North American Flatbed, Step-Deck, Lowboy, RGN and Extendable Trailer Makers (excluding MAC, East, Extreme, Heritage)

Research date: 2026-10-30. Method: I fetched manufacturer homepages and literature pages, downloaded the linked PDFs, rendered pages to images to confirm whether dimensioned drawings are present, and read PDF metadata (Creator/Producer/Title) for CAD-tool fingerprints. I queried Google Patents by assignee. Statements such as "I viewed the rendered page" mean I opened that specific PDF at the cited URL. A few manufacturer sites blocked automated fetching (HTTP 403/406/503). Those blocks are noted, and I did not try to get around them.

## Q1. Which manufacturers publish downloadable spec sheets or drawings with dimensions? (direct URLs and what each shows)

### Takeaway
The richest open sources are **Fontaine Specialized** (ex-Fontaine Heavy-Haul), **Fontaine Trailer**, **Landoll**, **Pitts**, **Trail King**, **XL Specialized**, **Lode-King** and **Eager Beaver**. Their public PDFs or images carry true dimensioned general-arrangement (GA) style side, plan or end elevations. Fontaine Trailer goes further and publishes title-blocked engineering repair drawings. The rest fall into two groups:
- Text-only specs: Talbert, Felling, Utility, the Etnyre brochures, and dealer sheets such as Hale.
- Nothing usable found publicly, or the site was blocked or down: Great Dane flatbeds, Wabash (Transcraft/Benson), Reitnouer, Manac, Doepker, Wilson, Load King, Nelson, Globe, Dorsey, and Kalyn Siebert (whose links are rendered by JavaScript).

### Inferences
- Openness ranking for drawing-quality public material, based on what I verified:
  1. Fontaine Specialized and Fontaine Trailer (Fontaine Trailer publishes real title-blocked engineering repair drawings)
  2. Landoll (versioned "LD" line-drawing documents with multi-variant dimension tables)
  3. Pitts
  4. Trail King, XL Specialized, Lode-King
  5. Eager Beaver, Witzco
  6. Felling, Talbert, Utility, Etnyre (text or photos only)
- The heavy-haul and lowboy makers publish dimensioned elevations more often than the high-volume commercial flatbed makers (Utility, Great Dane, Wabash). This fits buyer needs: heavy-haul buyers need swing radius, deck heights and kingpin settings for permits and loading.
- The drawings at Fontaine Specialized, Landoll, Trail King and XL look like simplified exports of 3D-CAD GA drawings that were then laid out in InDesign. The variant tables (Landoll) and the ride-height and swing callouts (Fontaine) suggest they are generated from engineering master models.

### Gaps
- Magnitude, Renegade and Xcalibur spec sheet URLs on fontainespecialized.com were not exposed (filename or "#" only). They may exist behind JavaScript.
- Fontaine Trailer's Build My Trailer page was not inspected.
- Not inventoried because of blocking, downtime or JavaScript rendering: Great Dane, Wabash, Reitnouer, Manac, Doepker, Kalyn Siebert, Wilson, Load King, Nelson. Manual or browser access would be needed.
- Dorsey's current operating status was not verified; only the parked domain was observed.
- I did not inspect every listed PDF page by page (for example the Utility 4000AE Drop Deck Features sheet, the Lode-King aluminum and combo brochures, the Pitts forestry catalog and the Trail King step-deck brochure).

## Q2. Does anyone offer downloadable CAD (DWG/DXF/STEP/IGES/SketchUp) or configurators that output drawings?

### Takeaway
None of the covered North American manufacturers publicly offers native CAD downloads (DWG, DXF, STEP, IGES or SKP). No .dwg, .dxf, .step, .stp, .igs, .skp or .zip links appeared on any page I crawled. The configurators found (Fontaine Specialized, Fontaine Trailer, Lode-King) are spec filters or price builders, not drawing generators. Generic, non-manufacturer lowboy CAD models exist on marketplaces.

### Cited Findings
- **Fontaine Specialized "Build My Trailer"** filters models by industry, capacity, deck type, length, height, gooseneck and suspension. It returns spec cards with "View Specifications" or "Request a Quote" links. It mentions no drawings, PDFs or 3D views — [Build My Trailer](https://www.fontainespecialized.com/build-my-trailer/all).
- **Fontaine Trailer** links a separate [Build My Trailer](https://fontainetrailer.com/build-my-trailer/) tool (not inspected). Several of the homepage "tool kit" links (Compare, Manuals, Training) pointed to a placeholder "sample-page" — [fontainetrailer.com](https://www.fontainetrailer.com/).
- **Lode-King "Build & Configure"** covers 11 models: Forceline steel, Renown combo and Brilliance aluminum flatbeds, drop decks and B-trains, plus hoppers. It has "View Saved Builds" and MSRP-from pricing. The page does not describe any drawing output — [Lode-King build](https://www.lodeking.com/build/).
- **Talbert** offers only a web Trailer Weight Distribution Calculator — [Talbert calculator](https://talbertmfg.com/trailer-weight-distribution-calculator/).
- **Gated material:** dealer portals at Trail King ([Dealer Portal](https://www.trailking.com/dealer-portal/)), Talbert ([talbertdealer.com](https://www.talbertdealer.com)) and Felling ([Dealer Access](https://www.felling.com/dealer-access/)). These may hold drawings; not accessed.
- **Generic third-party CAD (not from any manufacturer):**
  - DesignsCAD sells a "Lowboy Trailers 2D DWG plan" (about $3.87, metric) — [DesignsCAD](https://designscad.com/downloads/lowboy-trailers-2d-dwg-plan-autocad/)
  - 3D CAD Browser lists lowbed and lowboy models in STEP/IGES/DWG/DXF and other formats — [Lowbed Trailer](https://www.3dcadbrowser.com/3d-model/lowbed-trailer), [Goose Neck Lowboy 53 ft](https://www.3dcadbrowser.com/3d-model/goose-neck-lowboy-trailer-53-foot)
  - CGTrader has a 30-ton fixed-neck lowboy — [CGTrader](https://www.cgtrader.com/3d-models/industrial/industrial-machine/30-tons-lowboy-semitrailer-fixed-neck)
- **International contrast (partial):** Goldhofer hosts per-vehicle PDF sheets with dimension data (for example, used-vehicle sheets giving dimensions a and b and total train length) at goldhofer.com/vehicle-NNN-de.pdf — [Goldhofer STZ-VP sheet](https://www.goldhofer.com/en/usedvehicles?uitem=335), [example PDF](https://www.goldhofer.com/vehicle-285-de.pdf). Third-party Lectura Specs publishes dimension data for European low-loaders, for example the Goldhofer STZ-H at 795 mm loading height — [Lectura STZ-H](https://www.lectura-specs.com/en/model/transportation/trailers-lowbed-step-deck-semi-trailers-goldhofer/stz-h-11722250).

### Inferences
- In this segment, "blueprints" in practice means PDF or JPG elevations, not CAD. Anyone needing CAD would have to trace the vector PDFs or request files through dealer or engineering channels. The vector-based PDFs are the best tracing sources: Fontaine Trailer's NX-exported repair drawings and the InDesign-embedded line art from Landoll, Fontaine Specialized and Lode-King.

### Gaps
- I could not verify whether any European heavy-haul maker (Nooteboom, Faymonville, Goldhofer, Broshuis) offers open DWG or STEP downloads. My fetch attempts to Faymonville and Nooteboom download pages returned 404 (wrong paths), and searches found only dealer listings.
- I could not check GrabCAD or 3D Warehouse for manufacturer-authored models; search surfaced only community uploads.

## Q3. Owner's and parts manuals with exploded views or dimensioned diagrams (direct URLs)

## Q6. Load-planning and oversize-permit sources that use standard trailer dimension drawings

### Takeaway
No public load-planning or permit tool was confirmed to carry an OEM-specific North American lowboy or step-deck drawing library. The closest are:
- Transoft AutoTURN's manufacturer-specific vehicle libraries, which include a "heavy transport" category
- Bentley SUPERLOAD, Oversize.io and HaulClear for permitting and routing
- Third-party spec databases such as Lectura Specs

In practice, permit and engineering drawings rely on the manufacturer PDFs inventoried in Q1 (Fontaine, Landoll, Pitts, Trail King, XL), which give exactly the needed axle spacing, kingpin, swing and deck-height data.

### Cited Findings
- **AutoTURN (Transoft):** offers over 900 manufacturer-specific and about 1,800 standard design vehicles. It covers heavy-haul swept-path and clearance checks, lets users save custom vehicles to a library, and expanded its manufacturer-specific libraries to include heavy transport. The sources do not name specific lowboy OEMs — [Transoft AutoTURN](https://www.transoftsolutions.com/products/autoturn/), [webinar recap](https://www.transoftsolutions.com/civil-and-transportation/resources/blog/autoturn-basic-functionality-webinar-recap/).
- **Bentley SUPERLOAD:** oversize/overweight permitting and routing software used by agencies — [Bentley SUPERLOAD](https://www.bentley.com/products/superload).
- **Oversize.io:** axle-group weight calculators and state and province rule mapping for heavy haul — [Oversize.io heavy haul](https://oversize.io/solutions/heavy-haul).
- **HaulClear:** a permit-tracking SaaS whose Product Hunt listing mentions a fleet and axle library (unverified marketing) — [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/haulclear).
- **Lectura Specs:** a third-party spec database with dimension data for trailer models, for example the Goldhofer STZ-H — [Lectura](https://www.lectura-specs.com/en/model/transportation/trailers-lowbed-step-deck-semi-trailers-goldhofer/stz-h-11722250).
- **Dealer spec-sheet hosting:** Hale Trailer hosts many unit-specific spec PDFs for XL, Talbert and others under trailerimages.haletrailer.com/inv/docs/. They are text tables with kingpin, swing, deck lengths and heights, and carry an errors-and-omissions disclaimer — [Hale XL sheet](https://trailerimages.haletrailer.com/inv/docs/series/018383-018391-1.pdf), [Hale Talbert sheet](https://trailerimages.haletrailer.com/inv/docs/trailer/037603-2019TALBERT-1.pdf).

### Inferences
- For a permit-drawing or load-plan workflow, the most directly reusable inputs are:
  1. Landoll's variant tables (kingpin to rear axle, axle spacing and total length across 8 configurations)
  2. Fontaine Specialized plan and profile sheets (swing radius, kingpin radius, axle spreads)
  3. Pitts side and end views (loaded deck height and clearance)
  4. XL line-drawing JPGs (swing, kingpin, axle spacing)

### Gaps
- I could not confirm any load-planning product (for example Cargo Planner, EasyCargo or Load Xpert) with a library of named North American flatbed, lowboy or RGN models from these OEMs. Vendor library contents generally require a trial or installed software.
- I did not verify which OEM vehicles, if any, appear in AutoTURN's heavy-transport library.
