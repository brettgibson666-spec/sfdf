# Tracking Down Real Semi-Trailer Blueprints and CAD

None of the trailer makers covered here posts native CAD files (DWG, DXF or STEP), and none of the northeast Ohio builders (MAC and its brands, East, Extreme or Tremcar) publishes a dimensioned drawing of a complete trailer. Two kinds of real drawing are public. The first is **MAC's own nine title-blocked pneumatic-tank kit drawings** (part numbers 73803000–73803008, drawn 2015–2019) on MAC's aftermarket parts site. The second is **dimensioned drawings of whole trailers** from builders outside Ohio: Lode-King, Fontaine, Landoll, XL Specialized, Trail King, Pitts, Eager Beaver, Witzco and SmithCo. For MAC, East, Extreme and Tremcar, the trailer's geometry has to be pieced together from spec flyers that state dimensions in text, patent drawings (MAC lists about 80 patents), owner's manuals, and dealer build sheets such as Hale Trailer's per-unit PDFs. On software, **MAC's 2026 Design Engineer job ads require SolidWorks (2D and 3D) plus FEA**, while its 2015–2019 kit drawings carry a **Siemens Solid Edge** watermark; Extreme has advertised for Inventor or SolidWorks, Fontaine uses Siemens NX, and SolidWorks dominates the wider industry. The northeast Ohio lineage is documented from MAC onward: MAC bought Trailstar in 2013, and Extreme opened in 2016 inside **Tremcar USA's vacated Dover, Ohio plant** under Les Smith, Tremcar's former vice president of operations, while no public source documents people moving from East to Trailstar to MAC. "Heritage" is still unidentified after the checks described below, and because internal drawings marked as company property are confidential, the legitimate routes are public downloads, patents, dealer build sheets and measuring a trailer yourself.

## The best real drawings are MAC's nine kit sheets and eleven whole-trailer drawing sets

Two kinds of genuine engineering drawing can be downloaded publicly today, and neither comes from a MAC, East, Extreme or Tremcar sales brochure.

### MAC's title-blocked kit drawings sit in its aftermarket parts library

MAC's Aftermarket "Resources" page holds 216 PDFs, and nine of them are **real MAC engineering drawings**: pneumatic-tank retrofit and add-on kits drawn between 2015 and 2019 ([MAC Aftermarket Resources](https://aftermarket.mactrailer.com/resources)). Every sheet carries the same title block, which reads "THIS DRAWING IS THE PROPERTY OF MAC MANUFACTURING INC. AND SUBJECT TO RECALL AT ANY TIME. DUPLICATION OR DISTRIBUTION WITHOUT PERMISSION IS PROHIBITED." Each also has a tolerance block (inches; **angles ±2°, fractions ±1/16, decimals ±0.02**), drawn-by initials and date, weight in lbm, a revision table with ECN numbers, and exploded isometric views with ballooned bills of material. The title blocks name MAC Manufacturing Inc., which a c. 2020 MAC entity list gives as the dry-bulk pneumatic tank operation at 1453 Allen Road, Salem, Ohio ([MAC dump brochure, dealer-hosted](https://www.utilityne.com/images/pdf/mac_dump.pdf)), so the sheets very likely came from the Salem pneumatic engineering group. The tow-plate and hose-tube kits show real interface geometry for MAC pneumatic-tank frames.

### Repair drawings, floor installations and suspensions add more title-blocked sheets

Three other sources post formal drawing sheets for parts of a trailer. **Fontaine Trailer** publishes real repair drawings: its [Siderail Repair Drawings](https://fontainetrailer.com/app/uploads/2026/03/60c30e85c14a3a5e26dc7409_Siderail_Repair_Drawings.pdf) (9 pp) include a dimensioned splice plate in 0.375" 6061-T6 aluminum with weld notes under a "Fontaine Trailer Co., Haleyville AL" title block, and its [Winch Track Repair Drawings](https://fontainetrailer.com/app/uploads/2026/03/60c307c6f1ca72261db2721a_Winch-Track-Repair-Drawings.pdf) form a second set. **KEITH Walking Floor** publishes floor installation drawings and "Trailer Design Requirements" documents that trailer builders use to integrate its floors; the [Drop Deck Installation Drawings](https://www.keithwalkingfloor.com/download/keith-drop-deck-installation-drawings/) are a formal sheet with a zoned border, title block and isometric section details, and they download without a login after an ordinary browser visit (a request lacking the browser's session cookie is refused). **Hendrickson** posts PDF suspension installation drawings indexed by drawing number; its L1073 matrix, for example, points to D-25153, D-30900 and D-26595 for the 23K/25K/30K INTRAAX models ([Hendrickson L1073](https://www.hendrickson-intl.com/getattachment/23aa866d-e538-42cb-9699-5f570e235fe1/L1073.pdf)).

## MAC, East, Extreme and Tremcar publish dimensions as text, never as drawings

Its brands are MAC Trailer, MAC LTT (liquid tanks, now being folded into the MAC Trailer name), Beall (aluminum petroleum and dry-bulk tanks, Portland, OR), Trailstar (dump trailers), MAC Bullet (bottom dumps), MAD Trailer Leasing & Logistics, Aftermarket Parts and MAC Trailer Service ([MAC Family of Brands](https://www.mactrailer.com/our-company/our-family-brands); [NHTSA vPIC MID 5950](https://vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov/mid/manufacturer/details/5950)). ### East posts brochures, manuals and parts catalogs going back to 1989

East Manufacturing is now **East Trailers LLC** at 1871 State Route 44, Randolph, OH, and eastmfg.com redirects to east-trailers.com ([East Trailers](https://east-trailers.com/)). Its [Brochures & Manuals page](https://east-trailers.com/brochures-manuals/) offers 2025 brochures for every line, R3 operation manuals, 2025 parts catalogs, and archived catalogs as far back as a [1989 suspension catalog](https://east-trailers.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/Suspension-Catalog-1989.pdf). The 2025 Flatbed & Drop Deck brochure is photos and text with **no dimensioned line drawings**, but it gives hard numbers: the BEAST (BST) has a **10½" neck** and a 52,000-lb rating in 4 ft, the BST II a **13½" neck** on a main beam 2" deeper, the MMX a 13" neck, the BEAST Narrow Neck an 8" neck with up to 106" of enclosed clear height, and the XLD drop deck a **36" deck height**, all built on a dual-T main beam made from two 6061-T6 extrusions welded at the neutral axis ([PDF](https://east-trailers.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/East-Flatbed-Drop-Deck-Brochure_2025.pdf)). The 28-page [Platform Trailers Operation Manual](https://east-trailers.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/341128_East-Trailers-LLC_Platform-Trailers-Operation-Manual_R3.pdf) has load-distribution figures and torque values but no dimensioned drawings. East's suspension, hydraulic and pneumatic parts catalogs are its likeliest documents to contain exploded views, but they were not opened.

East's per-unit dimensions come from dealers. Hale Trailer's build sheet for an [East Genesis Unloader](https://trailerimages.haletrailer.com/inv/docs/trailer/072109-2020EAST-1.pdf) lists a 102" W × 50' L × 104" H body, 60"–60" axle spacing, a **51" fifth-wheel height**, a **36" pin setting** on a 3/8" plate, I-beam crossmembers on 12" centers, a 13'-3" overall height and a 21,062-lb empty weight. East's [dealer portal](https://east-trailers.com/dealer-portal/) is login-only.

### Extreme publishes web spec tables and a 75-page owner's manual

Extreme Trailers lives at **extremetrailersllc.com** (317 E. Broadway, Dover, OH) and should not be confused with extremetrailers.com, a boat-trailer company ([Extreme Trailers](https://www.extremetrailersllc.com/)). Its [XP flatbed page](https://www.extremetrailersllc.com/flatbeds) lists 45, 48 and 53 ft lengths, **neck transition depths of 8", 11" or 13"**, crossmembers at 14" and 18" on center, kingpin settings from 18" to 36", and load ratings based on a 48-ft trailer with a 24" kingpin and 122" axle spread. The [XS step-deck page](https://www.extremetrailersllc.com/step-decks) gives deck heights by tire size: **40"–41" on 22.5" tires, 37"–38" on 19.5" and 34"–35" on 17.5"**. The [2025 owner's manual](https://irp.cdn-website.com/3efefdf9/files/uploaded/Extreme+Trailers_Owners+Manual+rev.+2025.pdf) adds a 102" deck width, a 13.5"–15.5" ride height and a torque chart, but it conflicts with the website in places (it names a Hendrickson INTRAAX suspension where the site names Meritor, and one XP55 rating differs between the page's text and its table), so Extreme's figures should be treated as nominal. A 2017 trade article gives flatbed tare weights at 102" wide of **7,250 lb at 45 ft, 7,500 lb at 48 ft and 7,800 lb at 53 ft**, with a 54" deck height ([Trailer/Body Builders, 2017](https://www.trailer-bodybuilders.com/trailers/article/21742260/extreme-trailers-pushes-the-limits-of-light)). The "Brochure" heading on Extreme's [Resources page](https://www.extremetrailersllc.com/resources) has no link, and a dealer-hosted 2021 Extreme presentation now returns 404 ([Remorques Labelle PDF, dead](https://remorqueslabelle.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Extreme-Trailers-Presentation-March-3-2021.pdf)).

### Tremcar's website blocks automated reading, leaving dealer listings as the main source

None of Tremcar's own PDFs could be inventoried, because its site serves a reCAPTCHA "Bot Verification" wall to automated clients ([tremcar.com](https://www.tremcar.com/)); a human browser visit is the next step. Tremcar's NHTSA record lists WMI 2TL and models covering TC/DOT 406, 407, 412 and 423 cargo tanks plus sanitary, dry-bulk, farm-pickup and non-code tanks ([NHTSA vPIC 19927](https://vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov/decoder/Manufacturer/Details/19927)). The Ohio operation at Strasburg built **134 aluminum DOT 406 tankers in 2023 and 172 in 2024** ([Trailer/Body Builders, 2025](https://www.trailer-bodybuilders.com/whats-in-print/article/55284745/elemental-passion-tremcar-expands-us-operations)). Per-unit geometry comes from dealer listings seen only in search-result summaries: a DOT-407 stainless chemical tanker with a **66.375" outside diameter** and 25 psig MAWP ([TruckPaper](https://www.truckpaper.com/listing/for-lease/251443183/2026-tremcar-7000-gallon?print=1)) and a 316-stainless TC-407 unit measuring 48'6" overall ([TruckPaper](https://www.truckpaper.com/listing/for-sale/258310543/2027-tremcar?print=1)). A TurboSquid model of a Tremcar 7,000-gallon tank is an artist's visualization, not Tremcar engineering data ([TurboSquid](https://www.turbosquid.com/3d-models/3d-2026-tremcar-7000-gallon-tank-trailer-silver-2600789)).

### Heritage remains unidentified after five checks

No semi-trailer maker named "Heritage" turned up. NHTSA's manufacturer database lists Heritage Trailers LLC (doing business as Heritage Custom Trailers) at 10764 Industrial Park Rd, Benton, Illinois, registered for "Trailer and Marine Trailers" in weight classes 1A (≤3,000 lb) and 6 (19,501–26,000 lb), with production from 1990 and an ownership transfer recorded in 2024 ([NHTSA vPIC 23277](https://vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov/mid/manufacturer/details/23277)); those are light and medium trailers rather than 80,000-lb semi flatbeds, and heritagetrailers.com returned an empty page. American Heritage International is a holding company that agreed in 2025 to buy the Australian trailer maker Freightmaster, not a US builder ([AccessNewswire](https://www.accessnewswire.com/newsroom/en/banking-and-financial-services/american-heritage-international-inc.-announces-heads-of-agreement-fo-1073052)). The only "HERITAGE" trailer in auction records, a 1984 unit, was a dry van ([TruckPaper](https://www.truckpaper.com/listing/auction-results/246229175/1984-heritage)). Searches for Heritage aluminum flatbeds and drop decks returned only other brands, and no patents, spec sheets or software evidence surfaced under the name. A city, product type or era would let the next search close this out.

## Patents, dealer build sheets and a tape measure fill in the missing geometry

Extreme advertises "patented" 12" decking, but no patent number surfaced under its name ([Extreme flatbeds](https://www.extremetrailersllc.com/flatbeds)). Tremcar's key drawing-rich patent is **US11014486B2**, a tank-trailer operating system that moves cleaning, venting and loading devices to ground level, invented by Daniel Tremblay and carrying 13 figures ([Google Patents](https://patents.google.com/patent/US11014486B2/en)); an assignee search for Tremcar returns six patents and applications. For any other builder, Google Patents accepts assignee URLs such as `patents.google.com/?assignee=Fontaine+Commercial+Trailer&country=US` and classification searches such as CPC B62D53/06 (semi-trailers), which returns about 2,107 US documents ([Google Patents CPC query](https://patents.google.com/?cpc=B62D53%2F06&country=US)).

### Dealer build sheets carry per-unit dimensions

**Hale Trailer's document store** is the best single public source of unit-level build data for East, Polar, Travis, XL, Talbert and Trail King trailers. Its files sit at predictable paths (`trailerimages.haletrailer.com/inv/docs/trailer/` and `/inv/docs/series/`) and give axle spacing, kingpin setting, fifth-wheel height, crossmember centers and empty weight, under an errors-and-omissions disclaimer ([Hale East sheet](https://trailerimages.haletrailer.com/inv/docs/trailer/072109-2020EAST-1.pdf)). TruckPaper and MachineryTrader listings expose similar spec fields when "?print=1" is added to the URL ([TruckPaper MAC 39 ft](https://www.truckpaper.com/listing/for-sale/257526041/2027-mac-trailer-mfg-39-ft-tandem-axle?print=1)). Anyone drawing their own trailer gets the hard limits from regulation and standards: 23 CFR 658.15 fixes semi-trailer width at exactly **102" on the National Network**, 23 CFR 658.13 bars states from limiting the semi-trailer in a tractor-semitrailer combination to less than 48 ft ([eCFR 23 CFR 658](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/chapter-I/subchapter-G/part-658)), and the paid standard SAE J700 defines kingpin and upper-coupler geometry ([SAE J700](https://saemobilus.sae.org/standards/j700_201107-upper-coupler-kingpin-commercial-trailers-semitrailers)). Combining build-sheet dimensions, patent topology and J700 kingpin geometry produces a credible reference model without any OEM drawing.

### No trailer maker publishes CAD files, and model libraries hold only generic trailers

Crawls of literature pages at about 40 manufacturers found **no DWG, DXF, STEP, IGES or SketchUp download from any OEM**, and the configurators at Fontaine and Lode-King filter specs or show prices rather than output drawings ([Lode-King Build](https://www.lodeking.com/build/)). Public model libraries hold only generic work: GrabCAD's community-made, low-detail flatbeds ([GrabCAD example](https://grabcad.com/trailer.design-1/likes)), a generic STEP/IGES drop deck with a 44.5" deck height at 3DCADBrowser ([3DCADBrowser](https://www.3dcadbrowser.com/3d-model/dropdeck-trailer)), and, as the only "MAC dump trailer" model found, a $25 truck-simulator game mod ([Gumroad](https://kingcollinsmodshop.gumroad.com/l/qXqiV)). Component suppliers keep native CAD off the web too; at Hendrickson the route runs through its trailer technical-services team ([Hendrickson literature](https://www.hendrickson-intl.com/getattachment/1bf1d207-114b-48e2-8208-934bb7feb02d/L738.pdf)).

### Drawings marked as company property stay confidential

Several public drawings carry ownership notices: MAC's kit sheets (the "property of MAC Manufacturing" notice quoted above), Landoll's line drawings ("This drawing and all information therein is property of Landoll"), Fontaine's repair drawings (a proprietary-information notice) and Hendrickson's D-27367, marked confidential/loaned ([Hendrickson D-27367](https://www.hendrickson-intl.com/getattachment/00a499b4-1c21-4343-b7da-44f20b3a6b07/d-27367.pdf)). These sheets are posted for customers to view, but the notices reserve copying and redistribution. Internal general-arrangement and customer-approval drawings, which MAC's job ads describe as part of "engineering review of customer specifications and final drawings" ([MAC Kent posting](https://recruiting.ultipro.com/MAC1012MACT/JobBoard/c1bf9060-cc2a-41d6-9903-b2997377ff38/OpportunityDetail?opportunityId=179a07c7-94c5-45f6-95bf-7b46fa1fac52)), are not public at all and remain confidential company property. The legitimate routes are the ones described above: public downloads, patents, dealer build sheets and measuring a trailer yourself.

## Dump and tank builders keep geometry private, and federal rules keep cargo-tank drawings private

**SmithCo is the standout among dump builders**, publishing about 19 side-dump sell sheets ([SmithCo literature](https://sidedump.com/support/literature)); its SX7 sheet, for example, lists a 53' frame, 40' tub and 16" kingpin setting ([SX7 PDF](https://tawdry-bushbuck.files.svdcdn.com/production/docs/SmithCo-sx7-sellsheets-web.pdf?dm=1677101497)). Other dump and hopper builders offer dense tables but no drawings. Timpte's 2026 Super Hopper brochure has capacity tables by length, height and width ([PDF](https://timpte.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/2026-Super-Hopper-Brochure-072226.pdf)); Trail King's TKSSD side-dump page has an HTML table of frame and tub lengths ([TKSSD](https://www.trailking.com/products/tkssd-steel-side-dump/)); and Ranco's frameless high-cube sheet is a photo with a spec list giving 95 yd³, a 45' overall length and 102" walls ([PDF](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/65d945c49e17995575ffae2d/65e8eee13afda4fc51ba87ed_Ranco-Frameless-High-Cube-End-Dump.pdf)). For exploded views, Midland's 108-page bottom-dump manual includes a spindle view with more than 40 callouts ([PDF](https://midlandtrailers.com/wp-content/themes/midland/downloads/operator-manuals/Bottom_Dump_Trailer-Manual-2021.pdf)), and Etnyre's 57-page Blackhawk lowboy parts manual shows an exploded gooseneck assembly with ballooned parts ([MP-RTN-17](https://etnyre.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/MP-RTN-17_STATIC.pdf)).

Tank makers publish almost nothing dimensional. Wabash's tank one-pagers for its Brenner and Walker brands carry no dimensions ([Tank Trailer Overview](https://onewabash.com/docs/default-source/tank-trailer-downloads/Tank-Trailer-Overview.pdf)); Heil's brochure page returned HTTP 502, though a Heil petroleum owner's manual sits in NHTSA's archive of manufacturer communications ([NHTSA-hosted Heil manual](https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/tsbs/2016/MC-10166613-9999.pdf)); and Polar is documented mainly through Hale's 5-page build specs listing compartment splits and shell gauges ([Hale Polar PDF](https://trailerimages.haletrailer.com/inv/docs/trailer/015217-2017POLAR-1.pdf)).

Federal rules explain why tank drawings stay out of public view. Under **49 CFR 178.320(b)**, every DOT 406/407/412 design type must be certified by a registered Design Certifying Engineer, "the certificate must include the sketches, drawings, and calculations used for certification," and the manufacturer keeps that certificate at its principal place of business ([49 CFR 178.320](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/49/178.320)). The manufacturer must give the owner the certificates and, for ASME tanks, the data report ([49 CFR 178.345-15](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/49/178.345-15)), and owners must keep those papers throughout ownership plus one year ([49 CFR 180.417](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/49/180.417)). What is public is FMCSA's registry of cargo-tank facilities ([FMCSA cargo tank registration](https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/carrier-safety/hazardous-materials-safety/cargo-tank-registration-and-resources)), PHMSA special permits and interpretation letters, and NHTSA manufacturer records and bulletin archives, none of which contains drawings. **The one realistic channel for certified tank drawings is the ownership chain**, through which a tank's owner or buyer can obtain its certificate package.

The dump-and-tank research pass was stopped before it finished, and it leaves several gaps. The live FMCSA registry was never queried, so no CT registration numbers are confirmed for Tremcar, Heil, Polar or LBT. The example special permit, DOT-SP 14573 issued to Polar for duplex-stainless tanks, was seen only in a search summary ([DOT-SP 14573](https://www.phmsa.dot.gov/hazmat/documents/offer/SP14573.pdf/offerserver/SP14573)), so the run never confirmed how much design detail a public permit actually discloses. Transport Canada / CSA B620 facility registration, which governs Tremcar's Québec plants, was not researched; NHTSA Part 573 recall packets, which often include parts diagrams, were not checked; the Heil, Wilson, Travis, Doepker and Tremcar sites could not be read automatically; and KEITH Walking Floor's patents were never listed.

## Conclusion

A trailer maker's openness with drawings tracks what its buyers need. Permit-driven heavy-haul buyers get dimensioned general-arrangement sheets; buyers of commodity dumps, flatbeds and tanks get spec text, and MAC, East, Extreme and Tremcar all sit in the second group. For a MAC-, East- or Extreme-style trailer, a realistic "blueprint" is therefore a reconstruction that layers patent figures for structural topology, flyer and Hale build-sheet numbers for kingpin-to-axle, coupler and deck heights, 23 CFR 658 and SAE J700 for the hard limits, and a tape measure on a real unit, with the Lode-King and Fontaine Infinity sheets as models of what the finished flatbed or drop-deck drawing should look like.

Some of this material is time-sensitive. MAC is folding MAC LTT and Beall into the MAC Trailer name, and its 2015–2019 Solid Edge kit drawings are exactly the kind of legacy file that disappears in a website cleanup, so anything worth keeping should be downloaded now. MAC's move to SolidWorks brings it in line with the rest of the industry. For tank trailers, certified drawings exist by law but travel only with each tank's paperwork through the ownership chain. Identifying "Heritage" needs one more clue: a city, a product type or an era.
